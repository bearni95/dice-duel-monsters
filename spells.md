the 6 spells:

each spell rolls 1d6 when found, which determines it's X value


- trap hole: hide a trap on X red cells, callable on your turn
- shield +1: distribute X points of extra shield, up to 6
- sword +1: distribute X points of extra atk, no limit
- field card +x: all monsters with the attribute or type decided get +1 atk and +2atk and +1def
- wings: can distribute to X/2 (round up) monsters the flying attribute, which allows them to fly over creaature and empty cells
- energy: gain the d6 in energy


at duel start, each player receives 2 random ones, and another 2 are randomly placed on empty board cells
